# Vega Visuals Catalog

This folder brings together a set of custom visuals built in Vega for Deneb in Power BI. Each visual was created from prompts using different models and refined through multiple iterations to better match the expected behavior. It serves as a lightweight entry point: each visual has its own folder with full documentation, data requirements, and the corresponding JSON spec.

---

## Price Elasticity of Demand

![Elasticidad Precio-Demanda](https://sentidoanalitica.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/elasticidad-precio-demanda-v2.gif)

An analytical scatter visual for price elasticity of demand. It calculates elasticity directly in Vega, classifies each observation, and adds commercial reading through quadrants, KPIs, and trend support.

[Open folder](elasticidad-precio-demanda/README.md)

---

## Commercial Profitability Cockpit

![Commercial Profitability Cockpit](https://sentidoanalitica.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/commercial-profitability-cockpit.gif)

A profitability scatter cockpit for tracking revenue growth against SKU-quarter margin targets. It classifies each SKU into commercial quadrants, highlights margin risk and exposed revenue, and summarizes the visible portfolio with profitability KPIs.

[Open folder](commercial-profitability-cockpit/README.md)

---

## EWMA Control Chart

![EWMA Control Chart](https://sentidoanalitica.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/EWMA-Control-Chart-Params-v1.gif)

An exponentially weighted moving average chart for process monitoring. It is designed to detect small shifts in the process mean with dynamic limits and configurable control settings.

[Open folder](ewma-control-chart/README.md)

---

## SPC Control Chart + WECO Rules

![SPC Control Chart Preview](https://sentidoanalitica.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/SPC-Control-Chart-v1.gif)

A full statistical process control chart with WECO rule detection, zone shading, and capability support. It is built for fast interpretation of control states and rule violations.

[Open folder](spc-control-chart/README.md)

## Process Capability Histogram

![Process Capability Histogram](https://sentidoanalitica.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/process-capability-histogram.gif)

A histogram centered on process capability analysis. It combines the distribution, a fitted normal curve, and specification limits to evaluate Cp and Cpk.

[Open folder](process-capability-histogram/README.md)

## Population Pyramid

![Population Pyramid](https://sentidoanalitica.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/population-pyramid.gif)

A comparison-focused population pyramid that shows age and sex distribution across one or two years. It calculates totals, variations, and KPI summaries directly in Vega, and includes both English and Spanish versions.

[Open folder](population-pyramid/README.md)
